2. Ideas That Match Work You're Already Pursuing

Farmer and supplier engagement agent

An agent that works mainly over WhatsApp. It gives crop advisory and procurement updates, schedules collections, and handles supplier document submission and payment confirmations. SMS covers feature-phone users and email handles formal communication. The rural impact story makes it a strong candidate for the WhatsApp prize.

BFSI distributor and relationship-manager productivity agent

One reusable build for lending, insurance and asset-management distribution. It covers lead nudges, product Q&A and document follow-ups over WhatsApp, with daily email summaries. Because it works across several BFSI clients, it would make a good AWS Marketplace offering.

Other options: SalesAI for ITC Infotech, a retailer/distributor ordering agent for trade marketing and distribution, a guest engagement agent for hospitality, or an internal IT helpdesk agent.
